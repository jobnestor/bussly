Private sightseeing in Bergen with your own bus and driver

Bergen is Norway's busiest cruise port. A private bus lets your group skip the shuttle queues and see more than the harbour.

Short answer: a 4-hour private tour for up to 16 people costs around 6,050 NOK (€520) including VAT, or 9,300 NOK with an English-speaking guide.

See fixed prices for your group

Ideas for your tour

3–4 hours

Bryggen (UNESCO), the Mount Fløyen area, Troldhaugen (Edvard Grieg's home) and Fantoft stave church

7–8 hours

Day trip to Voss, Tvindefossen waterfall and the Stalheim viewpoint above Nærøydalen

Coming in on a cruise ship?

Questions

How much is a private bus tour in Bergen?

A 4-hour tour for up to 16 people in a minibus costs around 6,050 NOK (€520) including VAT, or 9,300 NOK with an English-speaking guide.

How far is Flåm from Bergen?

About 2.5–3 hours each way by road, so a Flåm round trip from Bergen needs a full day. On a short port call, Voss and Stalheim are closer.
