Private sightseeing in Flåm with your own bus and driver

Flåm sits at the end of the Aurlandsfjord. Many visitors only see the railway, but a private bus reaches the viewpoints around it.

Short answer: a 4-hour private tour for up to 16 people costs around 5,540 NOK (€470) including VAT, or 9,290 NOK with an English-speaking guide.

See fixed prices for your group

Ideas for your tour

2–3 hours

Stegastein viewpoint above Aurland and the village of Undredal

4–5 hours

Gudvangen and the Nærøyfjord, and the Stalheim viewpoint. The Snow Road over Aurlandsfjellet is open in summer only.
