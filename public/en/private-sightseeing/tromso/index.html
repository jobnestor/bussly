Private sightseeing in Tromsø with your own bus and driver

From September to early April, groups in Tromsø chase the northern lights. A private minibus lets the driver go where the sky is clear.

Short answer: a 4-hour private tour for up to 16 people costs around 6,300 NOK (€540) including VAT, or 9,550 NOK with an English-speaking guide.

Questions

How much is a private bus tour in Tromsø?

A 4-hour tour for up to 16 people in a minibus costs around 6,300 NOK (€540) including VAT, or 9,550 NOK with an English-speaking guide.

Is the night surcharge included?

Yes. Tours starting after 22:00 or before 06:00 include the operator's night surcharge in the price shown.
