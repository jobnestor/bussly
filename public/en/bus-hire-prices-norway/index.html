How much does bus hire with a driver cost in Norway?

Short answer: a 53-seat coach costs around 1,100 NOK per hour plus a 1,200 NOK call-out fee excluding 12% VAT, with a 2-hour minimum. A 4-hour private sightseeing tour in Bergen for 16 people with an English-speaking guide is around 9,360 NOK (€800).

Questions about prices

How much does it cost to hire a coach with a driver in Norway?

A 53-seat coach typically costs around 1,100 NOK per hour plus a call-out fee of around 1,200 NOK, excluding VAT, with a 2-hour minimum. Beyond 200 km a day there is a diesel surcharge of about 9 NOK per km. A minibus for 16 is around 850 NOK per hour.

What VAT applies to bus hire in Norway?

Passenger transport has a reduced VAT rate of 12% in Norway. A licensed guide is charged at the standard 25%.

How much is a private sightseeing tour by bus in Bergen?

A 4-hour tour for up to 16 people in a minibus with an English-speaking guide costs around 9,360 NOK (€800) including VAT on a weekday.
