Why a private bus on a port day

  • Leave when your group is ready, not when the ship's excursion leaves.
  • Your own route and stops, and no shuttle queues.
  • A fixed price for the whole group, shown before you book.
  • Back-to-ship planning: the all-aboard time is part of the booking.

Norway had 3,933 cruise calls in 2025, a record (Norwegian Coastal Administration).

Questions

What if the tour runs late and we miss the ship?

Enter the all-aboard time when you book. Bussly plans tours to end at least 60 minutes before departure and warns you if your plan is tighter. Drivers know the port routines.

Can we book a private bus for our cruise roll-call group?

Yes. Groups of 10 to 79 fit in one vehicle, and larger groups get several. Each guest pays less than on most ship excursions.
